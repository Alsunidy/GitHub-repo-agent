Two things in the report above are worth pointing at, because they are the
parts a reader would otherwise assume the model wrote:

The six "Update <package> from <old> to <new> or newer." lines are built in
code, not generated. backend/graph/report.py
(_deterministic_upgrade_recommendations) writes them from the fixed_version
OSV.dev returned for each package, so the version numbers cannot be
misremembered by a model. The model is told which packages are already
covered, and any recommendation of its own that repeats one of them is
dropped -- which is why no generic "upgrade these packages" line appears.

The three Security entries reading 'see "Top Issues" above.' are not
truncated output either: report_node condenses any finding it has already
printed in full under Top Issues instead of repeating title, detail, and
evidence a second time.
